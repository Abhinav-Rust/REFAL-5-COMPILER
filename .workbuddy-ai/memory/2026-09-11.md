## Bugs found by making the attempt (the pattern worth repeating)

Three of the four fixes above were *discovered* by trying to build the thing the
docs said was missing, not by reading the code. In particular:

- Refal-5 `s.` binds **any symbol** (char, number, identifier); `t.` binds any
  single term (symbol **or** bracket). `refal-runtime/src/matcher.rs` is the
  oracle; `refal-core`'s two matchers disagreed with it.
- A repeated variable in Refal must bind the same value, which makes LGG
  soundness a real constraint rather than a quality preference.
- Driving must distinguish a **cycle** (configuration on the active path) from a
  **repeat** (configuration that already completed). Conflating them breaks loop
  unrolling.
